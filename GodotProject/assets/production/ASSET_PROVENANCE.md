# Apptya Kingdom asset provenance and production notes

## Runtime models and materials
- Royal Captain and guard: generated by `tools/asset_pipeline/generate_models.py`. The authored meshes include layered armor plates, rivets, chainmail-like chest details, skirt/fauld panels, crown and weapon fittings, UV-mapped PBR maps, weighted armatures, and Idle/Walk/Attack clips.
- Pinned Quaternius RPG Character Pack meshes are retained under `source_models/` as CC0 reference assets; the generated Royal Captain and guard are the intended runtime character models.
- Watchtower: Quaternius Medieval Village MegaKit gate-tower model. Keep and Barracks are assembled from the kit's textured wall/roof modules; source PBR textures are resized to 512px for mobile.
- The asset generator checks required bone groups, vertex weights, armature modifier presence, animation clip presence and usable keyframes. This automated structural check does not replace manual visual review by a character artist/animator. The assets are an authored procedural prototype, not studio-grade AAA sculpting or motion-capture cleanup.

## Runtime audio
- The runtime kingdom theme is an original, procedurally composed 36-second stereo orchestral-style cue rendered by `tools/asset_pipeline/generate_audio.py`. It uses synthesized ensemble-string harmonics, brass-like swells, harp-like ostinato, a lead melody, low percussion and a short stereo room tail. It is original generated audio, not a commissioned orchestral recording.
- Runtime gather/build/train/victory/defeat/scout effects combine deterministic synthesized material-specific Foley-like layers with selected Kenney RPG Audio / Impact Sounds samples under CC0 1.0 Universal. Source OGG files are retained in `source_sfx/`. These effects are not a custom human-recorded Foley session.
- The CC0 track "Once Upon a Time (loop)" by TAD is retained under `source_music/` as an optional source/reference asset and is **not** the runtime score.
